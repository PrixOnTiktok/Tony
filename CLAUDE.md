## File revisions
Every new version of a deliverable is saved as a **new file** with a revision suffix — `_R1`, `_R2`, `_R3`, … — never by overwriting the previous one.

- The calculator lives in `deliverables/601102_R5 Calculator/` as `601102_R5_calculator_R<n>.html`. `R5` in the name is the Excel sheet's revision (601102_R5.xlsx); the trailing `R<n>` is the calculator's own revision.
- For the next version, take the highest existing `R<n>`, add 1, and also stamp it inside the page (footer, report footer and page title show "Calculator R<n>").
- Keep the earlier revisions in place.
